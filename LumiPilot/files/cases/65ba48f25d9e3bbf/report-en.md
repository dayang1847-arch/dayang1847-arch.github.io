## From pain points to verifiable product requirements

### RQ1 · After installation on the ceiling, it should not be necessary to take it down again for pairing

Evidence: R2 / R4

Requirement: The installation and pairing processes must connect seamlessly, and the device identity can be saved before installation.

Design suggestion: Use verifiable numbers on the lamp body, junction box, and packaging pairing card; scan the code to register first, then locate and group each lamp one by one; clearly indicate any missed devices.

Validation: Have a first-time user complete the multi-lamp installation registration for one room; record pairing time, number of reworks, and reasons for failure.

Boundary: Similar operational friction appears across sources and is worth verifying; there is no evidence quantifying its occurrence rate.

### RQ2 · Slim installation also needs to take visual comfort into account

Evidence: R1 / R3

Requirement: Both the seated posture in the living room and the lying posture beside the bed must be checked for the experience of looking directly at the light-emitting surface.

Design suggestion: Compare two structures—thin soft-diffusion and deep-cup shielding; retain the color-scene advantage and provide warm-white reading and a low-brightness night entry.

Verification: In the same room, compare seated and lying observation, light intensity distribution, low-brightness dimming and flicker; determine the evaluation method according to the installation environment.

Boundary: One historical glare feedback forms a hypothesis; it cannot directly claim that competitors fail or that this solution has UGR<19.

### RQ3 · Family members need a stable, understandable way to turn on the light

Evidence: R5 / R8 / R9 / R10

Requirement: The capability boundaries of wall control, App and voice platforms should be consistently visible.

Design suggestion: Select a verified control architecture according to the customer ecosystem; design commonly used wall control, network-loss prompts and recovery strategies; list single-light/group capabilities platform by platform.

Verification: Separately test external network disconnection, local network disconnection, power cut and recovery; record power-on lighting time and App/voice activation time; check single lights, groups and scenes item by item.

Boundary: The Govee community feedback is a self-report about an old model and cannot be mapped to this ASIN or used to identify a unified root cause; Hue already has a wall control module (S7).

### RQ4 · Before purchase, one can judge whether it can be installed

Evidence: R3 / R7

Requirement: Distinguish old-can retrofit from new cutout, and clearly explain hole diameter, ceiling depth and accessories.

Design suggestion: Design a Retrofit model and a Canless model separately; provide dimension drawings, cutout templates, accessory lists and old-hole cover options.

Verification: Trial-install with actual old cans, hole diameters and ceiling samples; record installation space and accessory conditions, then confirm the compatibility table.

Boundary: A product opportunity derived from positive experience; it is not a confirmed compatibility fault, nor does it promise one model fits all ceilings.

## Product Solution Draft · Comfort Connect 6 (working name)

Installs smoothly, connects reliably, looks comfortable, and family members can use it. Design the hardware, control, and installation process together.

### Installation and Structure

Prioritize 6-inch Canless versions; also set up a Retrofit SKU. Compare deep-cup shielding with slim soft light; do not promise one model fits all ceilings.

### White Light and Color Light

For white light, tentatively use 900–1200 lm, CRI≥90, and 2700–6500 K as discussion targets; measure color light brightness independently. Use 1%–100% as the proposed verification target for digital dimming.

### Installation Pairing and Control

The pairing card and device number must match; register before installation, and support per-light locating and omission prompts. Select the control route according to the customer ecosystem, and verify power-on, network-disconnection recovery, and voice grouping state by state.

### Feature Trade-offs

Retain color light ambiance; RGBIC backlight can be evaluated as an independent option. First verify white light comfort, low-brightness stability, and turn-on response, then decide features and cost.

The above are design directions or proposed targets; there are still no own-side sample lamps, measurements, certifications, or cost verification; sales leads do not prove a market gap, purchase intent, or our deliverability.

### First Confirm with the Customer

Target markets in the US / Canada / Mexico, installation conditions, required platforms, wall control, white light/color light priority, kits, and price targets.

### Then Move to Samples and Quotation

Measure optical, dimming, flicker, and thermal performance; check market-specific access requirements. List materials, control modules, processes, certification amortization, molds, and freight separately.
